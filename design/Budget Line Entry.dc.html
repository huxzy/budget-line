Motion: each circle runs its own 22–34s ease-in-out drift over a 4–9px path, alternating direction, with negative delays of 1–26s so no two are ever in phase. Targets move under 10px across half a minute, so a click never chases. Niger adds a 3.6s breathe and two rings offset by 1.4s. Hover on a live circle lifts it 3px and warms the fill in 160ms; press compresses to 0.96 over 120ms and releases over 180ms. Pending circles keep drifting and do not respond to hover beyond the tooltip, which fades in after a 350ms delay.
